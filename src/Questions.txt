    Bubble Sort Big O
    
    Question 1
    Worst case Big O time complexity of Bubble Sort is O(n^2)
    
    Question 2
    Bubble Sort has this time complexity due to it being a nested loop. Elements are sorted one at a time, and every cycle of sorting an element requires it to go through every single element that has yet to be sorted. In essence, each additional element adds a new cycle, as well as increasing the length of every cycle by 1.
